### 1.7 Structural Fork & Chosen Grammar: "Atlas Expedition Surface"
**Structural choice:** Distinct alternating expedition scenes on Web 1 linked via shared design floor and `VietnamAtlasStore` into Web 2 (`explore.html`) and Web 3 (`admin.html`), rather than a closed `worldflight` chain.

#### Why the 8 Built-in Grammars Alone Do Not Fit
1. **Filmic one-shot** bans jump navigation, multi-entry controls, and tool-like chrome (`"any chrome that implies the page is a tool; more than one entry point"`), which conflicts with a 7-pillar bilingual booking portal (`Hotels & Homes`, `Map`, `Flights`, `eSIM`, `Tours`, `Giftcards`, `Supports`) and multi-page CMS links.
2. **Live surface** bans full-bleed photography, kinetic display claims, and scrub video (`Bans: scrub, kinetic, spotlight`), eliminating the sunlit coastal parallax arrival and aerial peak.
3. **Continuous world (`worldflight`)** bans document `flow` sections, lateral `pan` rails, and operable panels over the canvas.
4. **Chaptered editorial** forbids full-bleed scrub peaks and functions as passive print reading rather than an interactive booking & CMS ecosystem.
5. **Typographic poster** forbids photographic grounds and cards.
6. **Gallery / catalog** forbids hero claims and restricts every section to passive museum labels.
7. **Split stage** locks the viewport to a 50/50 binary comparison until collapse.
8. **Rhythmic cutlist** bans `pin`, `dwell`, and `parallax`, stripping both hero spatial depth and the quiet pause before the peak.

#### Formal Contract of "Atlas Expedition Surface"
| Grammar Axis | Contract Definition |
